Build the Tenant panel (/tenant). Very basic and calm: a minimal, mobile-first layout with a top bar only (no sidebar), gold accents, generous spacing.

SCREENS
1. Home (/tenant)
   - Greeting with the tenant's name
   - "My home" card: unit image, unit name, address
   - Next rent card: amount MAD, due date, status pill
   - Shortcuts: My lease, My invoices
2. My lease (/tenant/lease)
   - Unit, start/end dates, monthly rent, deposit, landlord/manager name and contact
   - "Download contract" button
3. My invoices (/tenant/invoices)
   - List with period, amount, status (paid / unpaid / late), due date
   - PDF preview/download (the same rent invoice template as Phase 7)
4. How to pay (card on Home and on each unpaid invoice)
   - Manager's RIB with a copy button and the invoice reference
5. Profile
   - Name, phone, language switcher, change password

NOT ALLOWED
No chat, no repair requests, no uploads, no in-app payment, no other units' data.

EMPTY STATES
No lease yet → line-art illustration with "Your lease will appear here once your manager activates it."

BOUNDARY
Tenant panel only.
